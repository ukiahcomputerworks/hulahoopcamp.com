A co-created retreat

Come as you are. Bring what you love to share.

A small, welcoming gathering for flow artists, musicians, teachers, curious beginners, and longtime friends.

Days unfold through participant-led workshops. Nights open into music, illuminated flow, and dancing beneath the forest sky. Attendance is free, but space is personal and participation is intentional. Contact Kelly Viss before making plans to attend.

From the archive

This circle has history.

HulaHoopCamp has gathered at Barefoot Forest Homestead since 2016. The format has evolved, but the promise remains recognizable: free attendance, shared effort, daytime teaching, and room for everyone to take the stage.

2016

Two camps, one first year

HulaHoopCamp began with two gatherings in its first year. The surviving schedule includes workshops, opening and closing circles, teacher and student stage shows, an LED hoop jam, and a guest-DJ finale.

2017

The circle widened

Archived spring and fall programs added yoga, silks, fans, poi, buugeng, rope dart, group photos, and late-night flow jams alongside hoop workshops.

2023

Nine camps in eight years

The official HulaHoopCamp account celebrated its ninth free camp, evidence that the community-built model kept returning.

2024 to now

The tradition continues

The account marked its tenth camp in 2024. Current posts still call for teachers, nighttime jams, live music, and a place to practice, perform, and play.

Ask Kelly about attending

Good to know

A few first questions.

Do I need flow arts experience?

No. Curious beginners, longtime practitioners, teachers, musicians, and friends are all part of the gathering.

What does it cost?

Attendance is free. Contact Kelly before making plans because the gathering is small and participation is intentional.

When is the gathering?

The 11th annual gathering is planned for the second weekend of September. Exact dates will be posted after they are confirmed.

What should I expect from the site?

This is an off-grid homestead weekend. Bring your camping setup, layers, personal light, drinking vessel, and what you need to pack out your trash. Review the Arrival Guide before traveling.
