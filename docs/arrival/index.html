Before you leave

A little preparation keeps camp flowing.

01
Save the directions

Cell service may be limited. Screenshot or download the final route before driving.

02
Arrive in daylight

Rural roads and forest paths are easier to navigate before dark.

03
Pack for off-grid camping

Bring your sleeping setup, layers, drinking vessel, personal lighting, and everything you need to pack out your trash.

04
Use the community kitchen

Please do not create a separate cooking fire at your campsite. Shared cooking reduces fire and animal-intrusion risk.
